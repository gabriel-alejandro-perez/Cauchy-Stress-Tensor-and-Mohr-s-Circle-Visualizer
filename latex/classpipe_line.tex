\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Manages the order of 3D transformations (Pipeline) and the camera system. 

This class manually constructs the Projection (Perspective), View (Look\+At), and Model (rotation and scale) matrices. It uses quaternions to manipulate the orientation of the 3D differential volume element free of Gimbal Lock, and implements Spherical Linear Interpolation (SLERP) to smoothly animate the transition from any orientation to the principal stress basis (eigenvectors).
