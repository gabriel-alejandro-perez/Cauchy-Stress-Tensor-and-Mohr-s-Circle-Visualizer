\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Represents the Cauchy Stress Tensor and handles principal stress resolution. 

Contains the 3x3 stress matrix ({\ttfamily \doxylink{structtensor_a4d71fac9e5948d35cc619aa84f21fa56}{sigma}}), calculates the principal stresses (\$\textbackslash{}sigma\+\_\+1 \textbackslash{}ge \textbackslash{}sigma\+\_\+2 \textbackslash{}ge \textbackslash{}sigma\+\_\+3\$) by sorting the eigenvalues, and computes the maximum shear stress (\$\textbackslash{}tau\_\{max\}\$). It also allows computing the traction vectors on individual faces of the cube.

Computes the stress decomposition (traction) on a face of the differential element. 

Given the normal vector to a face and two orthogonal tangent vectors, it computes the traction vector using $ \mathbf{T} = \mathbf{\Sigma} \cdot \mathbf{n} $ and projects it into its axial (normal) and shear (tangential) components.

Diagonalizes the stress tensor to find principal stresses and directions. 

Invokes the Jacobi iterative solver ({\ttfamily \doxylink{namespacemath_utils_aae58b5ad2e568d065756d11852d250a9}{math\+Utils::\+eigen\+VV}}). Saves the eigenvector matrix in {\ttfamily \doxylink{structtensor_a49a4ba1c5893b4ffd28cb290ea8ac69e}{solut}} before sorting to preserve spatial orientation (preventing negative determinants), and then sorts the eigenvalues in descending order: $ \sigma_1 \ge \sigma_2 \ge \sigma_3 $.
