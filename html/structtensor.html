Detailed Description

Represents the Cauchy Stress Tensor and handles principal stress resolution.

Contains the 3x3 stress matrix (sigma), calculates the principal stresses ($\sigma_1 \ge \sigma_2 \ge \sigma_3$) by sorting the eigenvalues, and computes the maximum shear stress ($\tau_{max}$). It also allows computing the traction vectors on individual faces of the cube.

Computes the stress decomposition (traction) on a face of the differential element.

Given the normal vector to a face and two orthogonal tangent vectors, it computes the traction vector using and projects it into its axial (normal) and shear (tangential) components.
